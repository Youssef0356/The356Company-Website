# 06 — CREATIVE DIRECTION: INDEPENDENT WORLDS, ONE CONSTELLATION

> **Status:** Approved direction for redesign planning, November 5, 2026  
> **Experience format:** Continuous scroll-led digital exhibition  
> **Working concept:** Signal Atlas (the signal is a connective thread, not a repeated visual template)

## 1. Creative thesis

The website should feel authored as an experimental interactive experience, not as a conventional studio landing page. Visitors travel through four distinct product worlds. Each world has its own composition, palette, typography, visual material and motion language, grounded in verified product behavior and authentic assets. A restrained signal motif carries continuity between those worlds. At the finale, four independent signals appear together as a constellation: a visual expression of The356Company's future ecosystem ambition, not a claim that products already integrate.

The Signal Atlas name describes the journey's connective logic. It does **not** prescribe one appearance, one animation, or one product-section layout.

## 2. Experience principles

1. **Product first:** Every scene is built around a real, recognizable quality of its published product.
2. **Radical section differentiation:** Do not repeat the same header, split layout, card system, or entrance animation for all products.
3. **Motion with meaning:** Scroll movement reveals, demonstrates, or transitions. Avoid perpetual decoration and simulated telemetry.
4. **Truthful interaction:** Clearly distinguish a web interpretation from the actual app. Do not imply measurements, features, or game states that are not verified.
5. **Coherent, not uniform:** The studio palette frames the exhibition; individual products retain their own authentic identity.
6. **Visitor agency:** Native scrolling, keyboard operation, direct product links, touch input, and reduced-motion presentation remain first-class paths.
7. **Memorable restraint:** Use a few strong images and actions per scene. Complexity alone is not a design objective.

## 3. Studio opening and closing

### Opening — The field of possibilities

Begin in the studio's deep-purple visual space. The official logo remains untouched. Four distinct forms or signals emerge at different depths, hinting at the kinds of work visitors will encounter without presenting generic spheres or a conventional headline-plus-buttons hero. Typography should arrive as part of the composition. Establish plainly that The356Company makes independent applications and games, with a subtle invitation to enter the experience and an always-available skip/navigation path.

### Closing — One constellation, four independent products

Pull back to show each product's visual signature together without merging them into a fictitious shared product. A fine signal path may connect them as an explicitly future-facing sketch. State that the products operate independently today and that a connected ecosystem is an ambition. Finish with clear product/store destinations and studio contact.

## 4. Product art directions

These are distinct directions, not locked implementations. Each scene must be validated against current published builds and verified store assets before feature-level interactions are finalized.

### AR Explorer — Spatial instrument

- **Palette/material:** Cobalt, cyan, dark spatial depth; calibration marks only when they add explanatory value.
- **Composition:** A physical plane and real product interface become the focus. Potentially stage a phone and anchored object, as proposed in the original brief, using authentic screenshots or a verified capture for the screen.
- **Motion:** Scan, detect, anchor, inspect. Each movement should explain a verified AR action.
- **Avoid:** Calling a procedural orb, torus, or heatmap “LiDAR” or live AR telemetry when it is only a decorative web graphic.
- **Transition:** A scan trace resolves into the precise line of a document annotation.

### Pagelit — Quiet reading architecture

- **Palette/material:** Use Pagelit's actual interface identity; cyan may accent reading controls where supported by current assets.
- **Composition:** An editorial reading space with a real page/screenshot as the hero. Let whitespace and typography counterbalance the other, more kinetic scenes.
- **Motion:** Open, turn, select, annotate. A visitor may choose an annotation color if it maps to the released app.
- **Avoid:** Fake document content that looks like an actual feature demo, excessive floating pages, and unverified performance/privacy specifications.
- **Transition:** An annotation becomes a redaction or clue in The System.

### The System — A tense tabletop decision

- **Palette/material:** Noir tabletop, strong role-card artwork, controlled amber/crimson accents based on current published visuals.
- **Composition:** A hand or small spread of authentic cards, with enough real rules context to make the game legible.
- **Motion:** Deal, inspect, claim, challenge, reveal—but only implement a choice sequence if it follows the current game's verified rules. Otherwise make the card reveal editorial, not falsely playable.
- **Avoid:** Invented roles, outdated rules, or a “call bluff” button whose outcome is arbitrary.
- **Transition:** Card geometry becomes the first tile in Battle Cows' board.

### Battle Cows — Tactical pasture

- **Palette/material:** Use the game's real artwork and playful pasture colors, keeping its indie game character intact.
- **Composition:** Build from the actual board/sprites and a verified move sequence; use isometric depth only if it supports legibility.
- **Motion:** Hop, occupy, block, or capture only according to verified published rules. Use an accessible click/tap/keyboard alternative for any interactive move.
- **Avoid:** Static territory percentages presented as live game state, invented power-ups, or generic arcade effects.
- **Transition:** The board pulls back into the constellation, retaining its own color and shape.

## 5. Shared visual and motion language

- **Studio frame:** Official Deep Purple `#2C115F`, Light Purple `#DCB0FF`, White `#FFFFFF`; dark backgrounds remain restrained.
- **Product color:** Derived from verified product branding and current interface/artwork. Never recolor a product merely to fit the studio shell.
- **Typography:** Expressive display typography for scene titles; calm, highly legible text for facts and controls. Avoid packing every scene with uppercase HUD labels.
- **Continuity motif:** A fine signal/trace that changes material and meaning at each transition. It remains secondary to the product scene.
- **Motion grammar:** Spatial scan for AR Explorer; page/annotation for Pagelit; deal/reveal for The System; tactical movement for Battle Cows. Each product's choreography must be different.
- **Cursor:** Optional enhancement for pointer devices; never required to discover or operate content. Native cursor and visible focus remain available.
- **Sound:** No sound control or audio promise unless a real, user-controlled soundscape is implemented. Default audio remains off.

## 6. Content integrity gates

Before final scene copy or interaction is approved:

1. Compare claims to the current official store listing and, where needed, current published application behavior.
2. Prefer current Google Play screenshots over old promotional screenshots when they disagree.
3. Mark concepts, simulations, and aspirations clearly; never style simulated data as measured product telemetry.
4. Keep the public showcase limited to Pagelit, AR Explorer, The System, and Battle Cows unless publication eligibility is independently reverified.
5. Keep SendSnap and prototypes out of the flagship showcase while unpublished.

## 7. Design success criteria

- A visitor can identify each product's distinct personality without reading the same layout four times.
- Each product scene contains at least one purposeful, truthful visual demonstration.
- The journey feels continuous, but every product retains its own visual identity.
- The ecosystem finale communicates a future ambition without implying current integration.
- The experience remains understandable and navigable without WebGL, cursor effects, sound, or scroll-linked motion.
