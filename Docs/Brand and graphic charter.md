
# 02 — BRAND & GRAPHIC CHARTER

## The356Company

### 1. Brand identity

The356Company is an independent software and game development studio.

Its visual identity should communicate creativity, technological ambition, quality and innovation.

The brand must remain recognizable across its website, applications, social media and promotional materials.

### 2. Official color palette

The following colors are the official foundation of the brand.

| Color        | Hex     | Role                  |
| ------------ | ------- | --------------------- |
| Deep Purple  | #2C115F | Primary brand color   |
| Light Purple | #DCB0FF | Secondary brand color |
| White        | #FFFFFF | Primary neutral color |

These colors must not be arbitrarily replaced with alternative brand colors.

Additional shades and tonal variations may be derived from the official palette for gradients, backgrounds, shadows and interactive states.

### 3. Color usage

**Deep Purple**

- Brand identity and prominent visual elements.
- Primary buttons and important highlights.
- Dark sections and contrast backgrounds.
- Logo applications where appropriate.

**Light Purple**

- Interactive highlights.
- Decorative elements.
- Gradients and ambient lighting.
- Secondary buttons and visual accents.

**White**

- Clean backgrounds.
- Typography contrast.
- Spacious layouts.
- Product presentation surfaces.

### 4. Logo

The official company logo already exists and will be supplied as a project asset.

Rules:

- Never redesign or recreate the logo.
- Preserve its original proportions.
- Do not distort or arbitrarily recolor it.
- Use appropriate contrast depending on its background.
- Request alternative logo variants if needed rather than generating replacements.

### 5. Typography

The visual direction should use contemporary, clean and geometric typography.

Typography should prioritize:

- Strong, expressive headlines.
- Excellent readability.
- Clear information hierarchy.
- Controlled use of different weights.
- Consistent spacing and alignment.

Font selection must be validated visually before being finalized.

### 6. Graphic language

The visual identity may incorporate:

- Abstract geometric shapes.
- Dimensional 3D elements.
- Connected structures and nodes.
- Subtle gradients.
- Depth and layered compositions.
- Motion-based visual transitions.

These elements should support the brand story rather than become unnecessary decoration.

### 7. Product identity

Every published application and game has its own visual identity.

The company's official palette must provide consistency across the website, while individual product sections may introduce their own colors, imagery and artistic direction.

### 8. General design principles

- Premium rather than flashy.
- Creative rather than generic.
- Minimal but expressive.
- Consistent without being repetitive.
- Technological without sacrificing readability.

The final visual identity should be recognizable even without the company logo.
